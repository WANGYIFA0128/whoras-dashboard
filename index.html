Executive index methodology

Each index is a health score from 0–100. Ratings from 1–5 are converted to health points: 1 = 100, 2 = 75, 3 = 50, 4 = 25, 5 = 0. Index score is the average of mapped indicator health points within its group.

Interpretation: 0–20 Critical · 21–40 Poor · 41–55 Weak · 56–70 Moderate · 71–85 Healthy · 86–100 Excellent. Thresholds are illustrative, not validated norms.
